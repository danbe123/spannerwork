# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: April 5, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how SpannerWork may collect, use, store, and share personal information when you access or use the SpannerWork website, platform, applications, content, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

This document is provided as a starter privacy policy for the open-source SpannerWork project. It should be reviewed and adapted before any public deployment.

## 1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to personal information collected from people who use, visit, access, browse, register for, or interact with the Service, including seekers, providers, customers, maintainers, contributors, and support contacts.

## 2. Information we may collect

Depending on how the Service is configured and used, SpannerWork may collect the following categories of information.

### Information you provide directly

This may include:

- your name;
- email address;
- phone number;
- username and account credentials;
- billing and payout details;
- profile information;
- business or provider information;
- listing content, descriptions, prices, images, and availability;
- booking, quote, rental, and transaction information;
- communications sent through the platform;
- reviews, ratings, feedback, and support messages; and
- any other information you choose to submit.

### Information collected automatically

This may include:

- IP address;
- device information;
- browser type;
- operating system;
- approximate location derived from IP;
- log data;
- usage data;
- cookies and similar tracking technologies; and
- diagnostic, analytics, and performance information.

### Information from third parties

We may receive information from third parties such as:

- payment processors;
- identity verification providers;
- analytics providers;
- hosting, infrastructure, and security providers;
- communication providers; and
- social login or authentication providers, where enabled.

## 3. How we use information

We may use personal information for purposes such as:

- providing, operating, and maintaining the Service;
- creating and managing accounts;
- enabling listings, bookings, rentals, payments, and payouts;
- communicating with users;
- verifying identity, eligibility, trust, or safety information;
- preventing fraud, abuse, and misuse;
- enforcing platform rules, policies, and legal rights;
- improving features, user experience, and performance;
- conducting analytics and research;
- sending service-related updates;
- responding to enquiries and support requests; and
- complying with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, and security obligations.

## 4. Legal bases for processing

Where UK GDPR or similar laws apply, we may process personal data on one or more of the following grounds:

- performance of a contract;
- compliance with a legal obligation;
- legitimate interests, where those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests;
- consent, where required; and
- establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

SpannerWork may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies to:

- keep users signed in;
- remember preferences;
- measure traffic and usage;
- improve security;
- support analytics; and
- help operate platform functionality.

If required by law, the Service operator should provide an appropriate cookie notice or consent mechanism.

## 6. How we share information

We may share personal information:

- with other users where needed to enable listings, bookings, rentals, payments, communication, or trust features;
- with service providers and processors acting on our behalf;
- with payment providers to process transactions and payouts;
- with hosting, infrastructure, and security providers;
- with professional advisers, auditors, insurers, and legal counsel;
- with regulators, law enforcement, courts, or authorities where required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property; and
- in connection with a reorganisation, sale, merger, transfer, or open-source project handover, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell personal data in the ordinary meaning of that term unless expressly stated otherwise.

## 7. User-generated content

Listings, reviews, profile details, usernames, images, descriptions, and other content you choose to publish through the Service may be visible to other users or the public, depending on how the platform is configured.

You should not submit personal information that you do not want to be visible, shared, or processed through the Service.

## 8. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. SpannerWork may receive limited transaction-related information, but sensitive payment credentials may be collected and processed directly by payment processors instead of by the platform itself.

Users should review the privacy practices of any payment provider used with the Service.

## 9. Data retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- provide the Service;
- maintain records;
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations;
- resolve disputes;
- enforce agreements; and
- prevent fraud or abuse.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and the purpose for which it is used.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, but no system is completely secure. You acknowledge that internet-based services involve inherent security risks, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of personal data, communications, or stored content.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials and for using the Service in a secure manner.

## 11. International transfers

Depending on how the Service is hosted and operated, personal information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required by law, appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers of personal data.

## 12. Your rights

Depending on the laws that apply, you may have rights such as the right to:

- access personal data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- request deletion;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- request portability;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and
- complain to a relevant supervisory authority.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal exceptions.

## 13. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Service, contact the project maintainer so appropriate action can be taken.

## 14. Third-party services and links

The Service may contain links to, integrations with, or embedded content from third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. Their own terms and privacy policies will apply.

## 15. Open-source contributors and self-hosting

Because SpannerWork is open source, contributors, maintainers, and self-hosters may deploy modified versions of the software. This Privacy Policy is intended as a starter policy for the project and may not reflect the precise data practices of every deployment.

Anyone deploying or operating SpannerWork should review and adapt this policy to reflect their actual hosting, analytics, payments, logging, communications, and compliance setup.

## 16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we may revise the date at the top of the document and publish the updated version through the repository, website, or Service.

## 17. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, open an issue in this repository or contact the project maintainer through the contact details made available for the project.
